### More Glorantha Maps
© 2021 Mikael Mansen & Chaosium Inc.

Mikael Mansen is churning out more maps, with three more “generic settlement” maps (a [port town](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/382382/Glorantha-Settlement-4?affiliate_id=326228), a [scattered shoreline village](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/382477/Glorantha-Settlement-5?affiliate_id=326228), an [island settlement](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/382707/Glorantha-Settlement-6?affiliate_id=326228), and a [river town](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/382853/Glorantha-Settlement-7?affiliate_id=326228)), and a map of the [region around the Hellcrack](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/382481/Hellcrack--Glorantha-Area?affiliate_id=326228), a magical chasm up in the steppes of Pent where you can fall down to the Underworld.
