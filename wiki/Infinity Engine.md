## Conteúdo

### Starter Set Pre-gen Miniatures
![](https://godlearners.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/wip-infinityengine-starterset-pregen.jpg)

© 2021 Infinity Engine & Chaosium Inc.

Richard Helliwell of [Infinity Engine](https://www.infinity-engine.com/) shared this work-in-progress version of Makarios, one of the pre-gen characters from the Starter Set. The plan is that all those pre-gens are available in miniature form eventually! Infinity Engine already had the miniatures for the Quickstart adventure, along with [several other cool things](https://www.infinity-engine.com/index.php?id_category=21&controller=category&live_configurator_token=391e25d24fe83b03765f251daa9c4e56&id_shop=1&id_employee=1&theme=theme6&theme_font=).
