### Converting Gamemasters to RuneQuest With the Starter Set
*Guest entry by Jörg*

On Facebook, Sven Lugar [told about his seasonal gifting spree](https://www.facebook.com/groups/RuneQuest/posts/2137661053076549/?comment_id=2137693263073328), targeting 14 GMs of other systems with a gift-wrapped Starter Set. Of that sample, 10 are willing to try it out, one still needs to get his players on board, and three haven’t reported back yet. [And all of the non-GM friends who received one](https://www.facebook.com/groups/RuneQuest/posts/2137661053076549/?comment_id=2137693263073328&reply_comment_id=2138498189659502) were wanting to try it out, too.
