## Conteúdo

### Recent Well of Daliath Additions
[The Well of Daliath](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/) website is Chaosium’s online repository for Gloranthan Lore and RuneQuest clarifications and errata. It has some awesome content such as, say, an [index of all White Dwarf articles for RuneQuest](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/catalogue/publishers/games-workshop/white-dwarf-014/). It also has monthly archives of notable Facebook postings [by Jeff Richard](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/catalogue/websites/facebook/2021-12-jeff-on-facebook/) or [Michael O’Brien](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/catalogue/websites/facebook/2021-05-mob-on-facebook/), though without the commentary we provide here in our Journal of Runic Studies (which might be a good or bad thing depending on your opinion of it!)

The RuneQuest Q&A was recently updated with [clarifications of the sorcery rules](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/catalogue/publishers/chaosium/runequest-roleplaying-in-glorantha-players-book-print/cha4028-runequest-roleplaying-in-glorantha-qa-by-chapter/cha4028-runequest-roleplaying-in-glorantha-chapter-17-sorcery/) and the [errata for sorcery spells](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/catalogue/publishers/chaosium/runequest-roleplaying-in-glorantha-players-book-print/cha4028-runequest-roleplaying-in-glorantha-qa-by-chapter/cha4028-runequest-roleplaying-in-glorantha-chapter-16-sorcery-spells-qa/). Gloranthan lore recently saw [newly archived “Greg sez” articles](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/gloranthan-documents/greg-sez/), or detailed notes on [Sandy Petersen’s 1984 Campaign](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/gloranthan-documents/petersens-campaign-log-1984/).

There is a whole treasure trove of information in there… I’m a big fan of the [Prosopaedia archive](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/gloranthan-documents/prosopaedia/), which has the Runes of a lot of deities per publication, so you can see how their Runes changed (or not) across game lines. Another one I frequently consult is the giant [Third Age Timeline of Dragon Pass](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/home/gloranthan-documents/timeline-dragonpass/), which helps collate various sources easily. It is divided in several chunks of history, each with their own page. Note that it only draws on official RuneQuest and general Glorantha publications up to and including the GM Screen package, and ignores dates e.g. from any of the HeroQuest publications or from the Stafford Library book “History of the Heortling Peoples” or “The Fortunate Succession”.

David Scott is the librarian behind the Well of Daliath, but several volunteers help here and there with the website. You can [contact him to get involved](https://wellofdaliath.chaosium.com/want-to-get-involved/)!
