Greg Stafford Memorial Award winner [David Hall](https://www.chaosium.com/blogannouncing-the-winners-of-the-2024-greg-stafford-memorial-award-for-gloranthan-fandom/) has released an amazing new 130 page RuneQuest sourcebook on the [Jonstown Compendium](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/publisher/2/chaosium/category/34170/jonstown-compendium) — *Marsh Attacks*.

David is the leader of the wonderfully creative [Reaching Moon Megacorp](https://glorantha.fandom.com/wiki/Reaching_Moon_Megacorp) which, back in the day, published the fanzine *Tales of the Reaching Moon*, described by RPG historian Shannon Appelcline as "the most important publication for RuneQuest and Glorantha across its more than a decade of existence."\*

In recent years, David and the Reaching Moon Megacorp have had a resurgence, releasing best-selling Jonstown Compendium titles including [A Rough Guide to Glamor](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/309765/a-rough-guide-to-glamour), [A Rough Guide to Boldhome](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/467046/a-rough-guide-to-boldhome), and [Life of Moonson](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/334598/life-of-moonson-book-one-the-characters), and bringing back after 20 years [Home of the Bold](https://www.chaosium.com/blogcoming-to-chaosium-con-2024-home-of-the-bold-the-epic-gloranthan-freeform), the epic 50 person Gloranthan freeform game.

Later this month, David and the Reaching Moon Megacorp team are doing it again, returning with the 30 player Gloranthan freeform game [House of Malan](https://www.chaosium.com/blogchaosium-con-uk-update-reaching-moon-megacorp-returns-with-30player-house-of-malan-freeform/), at Chaosium Con UK.

![](https://www.chaosium.com/product_images/uploaded_images/screenshot-2026-05-02-at-9.36.21-am.png)

[Marsh Attacks](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/562474/marsh-attacks)  
David Hall and Friends ($15.95/#31.99, 120 pages, PDF and hardback)

Designed to accompany the upcoming Blackthorn Campaign *Little Swamp of Horrors* scenario book, this details the Lismelder tribe, the famous Greydog clan, the resurgent Humakti temple at the Six Stones and the Upland Marsh itself (plus a few ducks).

Included within are:

- Lismelder & Greydog history, demographics, genealogy and local spirits
- The Greydog Inn and its beers (plus brewing rules!)
- The Six Stones Temple, Indrodar’s Fort & the hero himself
- Monsters and monstrous plants of the Upland Marsh
- Local character generation (plus new duck occupations)
- Gloranthan Battle rules, adapted from Chaosium’s *Pendragon* role-playing game
- The Cult of Engizi
- Secrets of Vivamort
- Maps, tattoos, new skills, bagpipes and more…

![](https://www.chaosium.com/product_images/uploaded_images/screenshot-2026-05-02-at-9.42.36-am.png)

\*Indeed, on the current Chaosium team [Rick Meints](https://www.chaosium.com/rick-meints/), [MOB](https://www.chaosium.com/michael-obrien/), [David Scott](https://www.chaosium.com/david-scott/), and [Nick Brooke](https://www.chaosium.com/nick-brooke/) are all alumni of the Reaching Moon Megacorp — and many RuneQuest and Glorantha writers and artists also got their start creating for *Tales of the Reaching Moon*. Many of them are still active today, writing for Chaosium or in the [Jonstown Compendium](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/publisher/2/chaosium/category/34170/jonstown-compendium).

![](https://www.chaosium.com/product_images/uploaded_images/johnstown-drivethru-1200x150-2-.png)

The [Jonstown Compendium](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/cc/29/jonstown) is where you can find —and create —self-published material for your roleplaying in Glorantha with content for [RuneQuest](https://www.chaosium.com/runequest-rpg/), along with the [QuestWorlds](https://www.chaosium.com/blogannouncing-the-questworlds-srd-the-ruleslite-and-preplite-rpg-engine/), and [13th Age Glorantha](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/234486/13th-Age-Glorantha) systems.
